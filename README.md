## About StudyRoom

StudyRoom is a mobile app that helps university students find available study rooms on campus in real time.

- **Problem:** Students waste 20+ minutes searching for study rooms that are already full.
- **Solution:** A real-time availability app with booking and QR check-in.
- **Market:** 500,000 students in Kenya (TAM), 300,000 with smartphones (SAM), 25,000 at first campus (SOM).
- **Business Model:** Free for students, university partnerships, premium features.
- **Ask:** KSh 500,000 seed funding to launch in 2 universities within 6 months.
